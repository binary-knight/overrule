How a meeting works
Pick two to eight models. Each opens with an independent position, then the floor opens: members take turns, quote the claims they answer, raise objections with a condition that resolves them, and change stance only by citing what changed their mind. You can speak at any time and the next member must answer you.
The meeting closes by consensus, on budget, or as stalled. The drafter writes a candidate and the other members vote on that exact text; objections stay on the record. Unanimous agreement with no cited concession is labeled as no deliberation. Agreement does not guarantee correctness.
This app produces text and code in the browser and, with a workspace attached, can read or change a project on the host under the level you choose. Stop cancels pending calls; providers may still charge for work already performed.
Meeting history is kept on this machine (up to 30 completed meetings). Exports include your brief and all contributions. Keep the data/ directory private.
